Privacy Policy

Wishborne is designed for private reflection. It does not require an account, show advertisements, or collect your wishes.

Information we handle

Wishborne stores the following information only on the device where you use the app:

  • wishes you type or handwrite;
  • the ritual vessel and completion date for each saved wish;
  • the daily Wishlight balance used by the app.

This information is stored in the app's private local storage. It is not transmitted to Wishborne, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third party.

Private wishes and deletion

Turn on Private wish before choosing a vessel to complete a ritual without adding a copy to the Wish Archive. A private ritual still uses one Wishlight.

Saved wishes have no automatic retention limit. You can delete one wish or clear the entire archive. Deleting the app also removes its local data, subject to any device backups you control.

Optional App Store support

Optional support purchases are processed by Apple. Wishborne receives the selected product identifier and verified transaction status so it can complete the purchase flow. Wishborne does not receive or store your payment card details or Apple Account credentials.

Support purchases do not unlock features, Wishlights, or content.

No tracking or advertising

Wishborne does not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party advertising.

Children

Wishborne does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not provide public sharing, messaging, or user accounts.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date will be updated before the change takes effect. For privacy questions, email jaysonpan11@gmail.com .

隐私政策

Wishborne 为私密记录而设计，不需要账户、不展示广告，也不会收集你的心愿。

我们处理的信息

Wishborne 仅在你使用 App 的设备上保存：

  • 输入或手写的心愿；
  • 已保存心愿的仪式类型和完成日期；
  • App 使用的每日愿光余额。

这些信息保存在 App 的私有本地存储中，不会发送给 Wishborne、 广告商、分析服务商或其他第三方。

隐私心愿与删除

选择愿器前开启“隐私心愿”，即可正常完成仪式但不写入心愿档案。 隐私仪式仍会使用一枚愿光。

已保存心愿没有自动数量上限。你可以删除单条心愿或清空整个档案。 删除 App 也会移除本地数据，但你自行启用的设备备份可能仍保留副本。

可选的 App Store 支持

可选支持由 Apple 处理。Wishborne 只会收到所选商品标识和经验证的 交易状态，不会接收或保存你的银行卡信息及 Apple 账户凭据。

支持购买不会解锁功能、愿光或内容。

无追踪和广告

Wishborne 不使用广告标识符、分析 SDK、追踪技术或第三方广告。

儿童与联系

Wishborne 不会有意收集儿童个人信息，也不提供公开分享、消息功能或用户账户。 如需咨询隐私问题，请发送邮件至 jaysonpan11@gmail.com 。

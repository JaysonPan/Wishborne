Help with Wishborne

Quiet help, when you need it.

Wishborne does not require an account. Its rituals and Wish Archive work locally on your iPhone.

Email support Privacy policy

Where are my wishes stored?

Completed wishes are stored locally in the Wish Archive on your device. Open it from the book icon on the Vessel Alcove. There is no automatic limit on the number of saved wishes.

How do I remove a wish?

Open the Wish Archive to delete one saved wish, or tap the trash icon and confirm Clear All. The visible archive changes only after deletion is safely saved.

Can I complete a ritual without saving it?

Yes. Turn on Private wish before choosing a vessel. The ritual still uses one Wishlight, but no copy is added to the Wish Archive.

How do Wishlights work?

Wishborne provides ten Wishlights per local calendar day. Each completed ritual uses one. The balance refreshes on the next day.

A support purchase is pending

Apple may hold a purchase for approval. Keep your App Store account signed in and reopen Wishborne after approval. The app listens for the verified transaction and displays the thank-you screen. Optional support never unlocks features, Wishlights, or content.

Report a problem

Email wishborne.support@gmail.com with your iOS version and the ritual stage where the issue occurred. Do not include private wish text unless it is necessary to explain the problem.

心愿保存在哪里？

完成的心愿保存在设备本地的心愿档案中，可通过愿器藏阁的书本按钮打开。 已保存心愿没有自动数量上限。

如何删除心愿？

在心愿档案中可删除单条记录，也可以点击垃圾桶并确认“全部清空”。 只有本地文件安全写入后，界面才会移除记录。

可以不保存本次心愿吗？

可以。选择愿器前开启“隐私心愿”。仪式仍会使用一枚愿光，但不会写入心愿档案。

愿光如何恢复？

每天提供十枚愿光，每次完成仪式使用一枚，并在下一个本地自然日恢复。

支持购买仍在等待

Apple 可能要求批准购买。请保持 App Store 账户登录，批准后重新打开 Wishborne。App 会接收经验证的交易并显示感谢页面。可选支持不会解锁 功能、愿光或内容。

反馈问题

请发送邮件至 wishborne.support@gmail.com ，并附上 iOS 版本和出现问题的仪式阶段。除非说明问题确有必要， 请勿附上隐私心愿内容。
